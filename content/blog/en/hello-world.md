## Why VNDR

My Chinese name sounds a lot like the English word **vendor**. Then I removed the e and n from vendor, leaving **VNDR** — it still reads the same.

Trim it further and you get **VD**, which became my logo.

## What this site is

VNDR is not a single-topic blog. It is a personal website integration project. Blog posts, hobby development, Now, bookmarks, and anything I want later can grow here as modules.

For now, there is no account system or auth. It is a read-only content site: to change content, edit the project files and MDX directly.
